Peanut butter and Jelly

This is a pretty easy recipe to remember.

This may be the easiest bit of food you will ever make.

Ingredients

  • Peanut butter of your choice
  • Jelly of your choice
  • Bread of your choice

Steps

  1. Spread peanut butter evenly on one piece of bread
  2. Spread jelly evenly on one piece of bread
  3. Put the peanut butter and the jelly side together
  4. Enjoy your sandwich
